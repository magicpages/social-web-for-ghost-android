Your Ghost site on the fediverse, right from your phone.

Ghost 6 connects your site to the open social web (ActivityPub): people on Mastodon, WordPress, other Ghost sites and the rest of the fediverse can follow you, and you can follow them. Social Web is a fast, native way to keep up and join in.

• Notes: short posts from everyone you follow. Like, repost, quote and reply. The feed keeps your place, shows a “new posts” button instead of jumping, and marks where you’re caught up.
• Feed filters: hide reposts and posts with words you mute.
• Reader: long-form articles from the publications you follow, in a clean reading view with audio and video.
• Post notes with photos and alt text, from your site’s own profile, or take a photo right in the composer.
• Edit your profile: name, username, bio, picture and cover image.
• Share any profile as a QR code, a link or a picture card.
• Notifications: likes, reposts, replies, mentions and new followers, neatly grouped. Choose which ones are announced in the background.
• Translate notes and articles into your language, on your device.
• Find and follow people and publications on any fediverse server, or browse topics like News or Photography.
• Share your site on Bluesky through Bridgy Fed.
• Choose whether media marked as sensitive shows without a tap.
• All your Ghost sites in one app. Switch between them in a tap.
• Share a link, selected text or a photo from any app to start a new note (one image, with alt text).
• Animated GIFs and other animated images play right in the timeline.
• Link preview cards in notes and while you write.
• On tablets, foldables and in landscape, a thread or profile opens beside the timeline.
• Members: sign in as a member of a site to read its members-only posts.
• Recommend other sites to your readers, right from their profile.
• Block accounts or whole servers, and report posts and people.
• Colors from your site’s accent color, your wallpaper or a color you pick.

Requirements: a Ghost 6 site with the social web turned on, and an Owner or Administrator account on it. You sign in to your own site; there’s no new account to create.
